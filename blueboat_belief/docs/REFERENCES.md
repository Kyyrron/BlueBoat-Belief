# REFERENCES: what each source supports, and whether it was read

**Rule:** cite nothing from memory. Every non-software choice in `MATH.md` / `DESIGN.md` traces to
a row here. A source that is relevant to an idea is not evidence for a numerical parameter.

Status values:
- **PRIMARY**: the full text was read in a module session; claims point to sections and equations.
- **RECORD**: the bibliographic record was confirmed on the web on 2026-11-12 (title, authors,
  venue, pages, DOI); the text was **not** read in a module session. Such a source may be cited
  for what its abstract states and for nothing else.
- **NOTES**: known only through the project's `literature_conclusions.md` or the brief.
- **NOT READ**.

The PDFs of Paull 2013, Bourgault 2006, Fawcett 2010 and Cobra 1992 are on the operator laptop
(`~/Downloads/research_paper/`). Paull and Bourgault were re-checked there on 2026-11-12.
